# Israel: air monitoring and public information responsibilities

## PM2.5 and PM10: monitoring and public information

The Ministry of Environmental Protection's air-pollution department operates air monitoring and sampling, publishes air-quality information, and provides public guidance in high-pollution conditions.

For this corpus's PM2.5/PM10 scope, the corresponding decision-support recommendation is:

Use official air-quality monitoring information and follow Ministry of Environmental Protection public updates.

The environmental-protection authority owns these functions. This organizational remit specifies no anomaly-response deadline or priority, deployment, staffing, instrument quantity or pollutant threshold. It does not establish that a particular authority has acted on this anomaly. Preliminary readings remain preliminary.
