# Ministry of Education Field Trip Flood Safety Procedure

Source: Ministry of Education (Israel) - National Safety Department[cite: 13]
Status: Official operational procedure[cite: 13]
Note: Translated and adapted from the original Hebrew document[cite: 13].

## Trip Coordination and Weather Monitoring

* The final decision to close or open a specific route is made by the Situation Room (Cheder Matzav)[cite: 13]. This decision is based on the wadi's characteristics, the hikers' ability to self-rescue during the hike, and the severity of the flash flood alert (light, moderate, or heavy)[cite: 13].
* For routes designated as prone to flash floods, the Trip Person-in-Charge (PIC) representing the school or youth movement must proactively contact the Situation Room to check the forecast and obtain approval on the evening before the trip[cite: 13].
* The PIC is strictly required to contact the Situation Room again on the morning of the trip to receive final approval prior to entering the wadi[cite: 13].

## Wadi Entry and Flash Flood Thresholds

* The Situation Room continuously monitors weather forecasting technologies capable of estimating precipitation amounts, timelines, and specific flash flood locations[cite: 13].
* If weather conditions or alert severities change after the initial approval has been granted, and there is even the slightest risk of a flash flood, the Situation Room is obligated to contact the PIC, notify them of the danger, and strictly forbid entry to the wadi[cite: 13].

## Emergency Evacuation and Unreachable Groups

* In the event that a flash flood risk emerges and the Situation Room is unable to establish communication with the hiking group, a Trip Inspector (Pakach Tiulim) will be immediately dispatched to the field[cite: 13].
* The Trip Inspector's mandate is to physically locate the hikers and evacuate them from the danger zone[cite: 13].

## Night Camps and Sleepover Evacuation

* If groups are sleeping in field campgrounds located in flood-prone areas (such as Nahal Nekarot, Nahal Tze'elim, or Nahal Mishmar) and a flash flood alert is issued, the Situation Room must proactively initiate contact with the PIC[cite: 13].
* The Situation Room will update the PIC regarding the impending danger and mandate an immediate evacuation of the campground[cite: 13].
