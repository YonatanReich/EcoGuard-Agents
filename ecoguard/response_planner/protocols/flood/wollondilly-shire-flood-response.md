# Wollondilly Shire Flood Emergency Response Procedure

Source: Wollondilly Shire Council & NSW State Emergency Service[cite: 22]
Status: Official operational procedure (Foreign Reference)[cite: 22]

## Applicability to Israel

This document is a foreign operational reference. It may support rescue
technique and responder-safety decisions, but it does not define legal
authority, agency responsibility, evacuation powers, or binding road-closure
thresholds in Israel. Israeli procedures take precedence.

## Flood Warning and Activation Thresholds

* Flood response operations begin upon receipt of severe weather warnings, flood watches/warnings, flash flood warnings, or dam failure alerts[cite: 22].
* **Dam Failure Red Alert Thresholds (Reference)**:
  * Triggered at Full Supply Level (FSL) + 2.5m to 3.0m and rising for standard dams (e.g., Avon, Cataract, Cordeaux)[cite: 22].
  * Triggered at FSL + 6.0m to 13.0m for extreme capacity dams (e.g., Nepean, Warragamba)[cite: 22].

## Road Closure Criteria

* **Douglas Park Drive**: Cut at the Nepean Causeway at a gauge height of 2.5m[cite: 22].
* **Menangle Road Bridge**: Deck height is 5.2m; overtopping signifies the start of Minor flooding for downstream areas[cite: 22].
* **Bents Basin Road**: Cut early during flooding near its junction with Silverdale Road, isolating all properties to the south[cite: 22].

## Evacuation Protocols

* Evacuations must take place when homes or businesses are likely to flood, when people are unsuited to living in isolated circumstances, or when essential energy/utility services are likely to fail[cite: 22].
* **Health Facilities**: The evacuation of hospitals, health centres, and aged care facilities should be coordinated in consultation with emergency and welfare services[cite: 22].
* **Schools**: School administration offices coordinate the evacuation of schools[cite: 22].

## Flowing-Water and Flood Rescue Guidelines

* **Primary Rescue**: Flood rescues should only be performed where a risk assessment indicates the risk to rescuers is acceptable[cite: 22].
* **Animal Rescue**: Animal welfare operations include the rescue and emergency care of livestock, pets, and wildlife[cite: 22].

## Post-Incident Return and All Clear

* Determinations on when it is safe to progressively return must consider: access, communications, power, gas, infrastructure damage, hazardous materials, and public health risks[cite: 22].
* Return access is categorized as[cite: 22]: 
  1. Not suitable for access[cite: 22].
  2. Limited access by emergency services[cite: 22].
  3. Limited access by residents/business operators[cite: 22].
  4. Full access[cite: 22].
