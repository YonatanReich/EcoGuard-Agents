# Coastal Authorities Extreme Rainfall Reference Scenarios

Source: Israel Meteorological Service (IMS), Ministry of Transport[cite: 19]
Status: Official national reference scenario[cite: 19]
Note: Translated and adapted from the original Hebrew document[cite: 19].

## General Flood Warning and Alert Lead Times

* **Initial Alert**: Issued 3 to 5 days in advance based on the potential for significant precipitation on saturated ground[cite: 19].
* **Joint Preliminary Alert**: The IMS and the Water Authority jointly issue a preliminary alert for river overflows at least 72 hours prior to the event[cite: 19].
* **Focused Alert**: Issued 24 to 48 hours prior to the event, detailing the start time, expected duration, and severity[cite: 19].
* **Unpredictability Constraint**: Localized intense rain cells (e.g., in the Carmel or Gush Dan areas) are highly unpredictable; pinpointing the exact location of extreme downpours is often limited to a short-term window of up to 24 hours[cite: 19].

## Region 1: Northern Coastal Plain (Nahariya, Gaaton, Yasaf)

* **Threshold**: 100mm or more of continuous rain over 12–36 hours, falling on saturated ground with pre-existing base flows[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Rapid rise of Western Galilee streams[cite: 19]. 
  * Closure of Highway 4 (between Acre and Nahariya) due to flooding from the Yasaf and Beit HaEmek streams[cite: 19].
  * Inundation of Gaaton Blvd and eastern Nahariya neighborhoods[cite: 19].
  * Isolation of settlements north and east of Nahariya (due to Kziv and Betzet streams)[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Widespread home evacuations and rescues of trapped individuals from vehicles on blocked routes[cite: 19].

## Region 2: Haifa Bay (Kishon, Gdora, Naaman)

* **Threshold**: Cumulative rainfall exceeding 200mm over 2–5 days on saturated ground, combined with an initial burst of 50–100mm within a few hours[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Widespread flooding in the Zevulun Valley, the Krayot, Check Post area, and Lower City[cite: 19].
  * Coastal streets in Bat Galim flooded due to high waves and seawater intrusion[cite: 19].
  * Severe transportation disruptions in commercial/industrial zones[cite: 19].
  * Extended power outages due to lightning strikes and falling trees[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Large-scale home evacuations in the Krayot area and clearance of heavy mud/debris from intercity routes[cite: 19].

## Region 3: Carmel and Slopes (Haifa West)

* **Threshold**: Extreme localized intensity of 50mm+ in one hour, or 100mm+ within 3–4 hours[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Immediate drainage system failure causing water to overflow from drainage grates[cite: 19].
  * Flooding at Check Post, Lower City, Hagana Road, Carmel Coast, and Highway 4 near Kfar Galim[cite: 19].
  * Heavy mud and debris accumulating on routes adjacent to steep slopes[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Immediate rescues from flooded streets and clearance of blocked railway tracks[cite: 19].

## Region 4: Sharon Region

* **Threshold**: Cumulative rainfall >200mm, with nearly 100mm falling in a single day on saturated ground[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Widespread flooding of open areas due to overflowing Shomron streams[cite: 19].
  * Severe urban flooding[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Rescues at stream crossings and flooded streets, and management of route blockages[cite: 19].

## Region 5: Gush Dan (Ayalon, Yarkon)

* **Threshold**: Total event rainfall of 250–350mm, with half of the amount falling in less than 24 hours[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Inundation of low-lying neighborhoods in Southern Tel Aviv and Jaffa[cite: 19].
  * Blockage of the Ayalon Highway and complete halt of train traffic[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Critical, life-saving rescues from flooded basements, underground parking lots, and ground-floor apartments[cite: 19].

## Region 6: Southern Coastal Plain (Ashdod, Ashkelon, Lachish, Evtach)

* **Threshold**: 100–150mm of daily rainfall concentrated on saturated and sealed urban ground[cite: 19].
* **Expected Impacts**: 
  * Flooding in the low neighborhoods of Ashdod (Rova 6, Rova 8, Northern Industrial Zone), southern Gan Yavne, Givati, Emunim, Nir Yisrael, Beit Shikma, Gea, and northern Be'er Ganim[cite: 19].
  * Highway 4 blocked at Ad Halom Interchange, cutting off settlements to the east[cite: 19].
* **Operational Requirements**: Rescues from basements, flooded apartments, and trapped vehicles[cite: 19].
