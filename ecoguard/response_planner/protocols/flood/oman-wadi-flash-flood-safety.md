# Oman Meteorology Wadi Flash Flood Safety Guidelines

Source: Directorate General of Meteorology, Civil Aviation Authority (Oman)[cite: 17]
Status: Official operational safety guidance (Foreign Reference)[cite: 17]

## Applicability to Israel

This document is a foreign operational reference. It may support rescue
technique and responder-safety decisions, but it does not define legal
authority, agency responsibility, evacuation powers, or binding road-closure
thresholds in Israel. Israeli procedures take precedence.

## Wadi and Flash Flood Characteristics

* Flash floods and wadi flows can develop rapidly following heavy rainfall over a short period of time[cite: 17]. 
* These events can become life-threatening within minutes, particularly in wadis and low-lying areas[cite: 17].

## Water Depth Safety Thresholds (Flowing Water)

When assessing the risk of crossing or operating in flowing wadi water, responders and civilians must adhere to the following physical thresholds[cite: 17]:

* **~15 cm (About 6 inches)**: Fast-flowing water at this depth is sufficient to knock an adult off their feet and carry them away[cite: 17]. 
* **~30 cm (About 12 inches)**: Fast-flowing water at this depth can sweep away a small car[cite: 17].
* **~45–60 cm (About 18–24 inches)**: Fast-flowing water at this depth is capable of sweeping away most SUVs and trucks[cite: 17].
