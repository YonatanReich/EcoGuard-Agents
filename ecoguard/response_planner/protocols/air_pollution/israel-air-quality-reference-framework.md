# Israel: air-quality reference framework

## Israeli values and WHO target-value update

In an official public-consultation notice, Israel's Ministry of Environmental Protection described proposed updates to Clean Air quality-value regulations for PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2, SO2 and CO, including adoption of updated WHO health-based target values. The notice distinguishes health-based target values from environmental values and the legal process for updating them.

This corpus records that Israeli target, environmental and alert values are distinct concepts. It supplies no numeric value and authorizes no response action. A current effective Israeli legal text and the correct averaging period are required before any legal comparison. WHO levels are health-based guidelines and are not automatically Israeli legal limits.
