# US EPA AirNow: ambient carbon-monoxide public-health guidance

## Ambient CO: US AQI Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

The US EPA AQI technical assistance document identifies people with heart disease as the group most at risk from ambient CO. At the US AQI category "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" (101-150), it advises people with heart disease, such as angina, to limit heavy exertion and avoid CO sources such as heavy traffic.

The reviewed international-reference recommendation is:

If an authorized source explicitly reports ambient CO using the US EPA AQI category Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, communicate the matching US AirNow advice that people with heart disease limit heavy exertion and avoid high-CO settings such as heavy traffic.

This applies only to outdoor ambient-monitoring context. Indoor carbon-monoxide poisoning guidance is outside scope; this document does not support diagnosing poisoning, entering a building, ventilation instructions or appliance actions. Do not infer US AQI from Israeli AQI, EcoGuard severity, a raw concentration or another standard. Nearby traffic does not establish source attribution. No evacuation, road closure or dispatch is supported.
