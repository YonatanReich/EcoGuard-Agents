# Uncorroborated report planner

What to do with a report that nothing has confirmed - somebody posted that
there is smoke, and no satellite, gauge or station agrees.

There is nothing measured here to analyse, so these skip the analyzers
entirely. The advice is who to contact: the police station responsible for that
area, with its phone number, and the local authority.

No model is involved and nothing is dispatched. The report is passed on as what
it is - an unconfirmed report - and a person decides.
