# Israel: particulate-pollution public-health advisory

## PM2.5 and PM10: conditional protective advice

During the 11 May 2024 dust episode, the environment and health ministries advised vulnerable groups (heart/lung patients, older adults, children and pregnant people) to avoid strenuous outdoor exercise; others were advised to reduce it. Authorities would update the public using forecasts and station readings.

For PM2.5/PM10 planning, this supports the following conditional recommendation:

If authorities confirm an applicable severe particulate-pollution advisory, communicate its advice: vulnerable groups avoid strenuous outdoor activity; others reduce it.

This historical dust advisory does not establish current conditions, dust causation, exposure, numeric triggers, deadlines or actions for gases. Anomaly severity is not an official advisory category. Obtain current authority confirmation before applying the conditional advice.
