# US EPA National Ambient Air Quality Standards reference

## US regulatory scope

The US EPA NAAQS table lists primary public-health and secondary public-welfare standards with pollutant-specific averaging times and regulatory forms for criteria pollutants, including PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2, SO2 and ambient CO. Compliance forms include multi-year statistics for several pollutants and cannot be replaced by a single raw observation.

These are United States regulatory standards. They are not Israeli legal thresholds, WHO guideline values, US AQI action categories or direct response instructions. This document authorizes no action and includes no threshold values; a legal comparison requires the current EPA table and its full averaging/form requirements.
