# WHO global air-quality guidelines 2021

## Health-based reference scope

The World Health Organization's 2021 global air-quality guidelines cover PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2, SO2 and CO. WHO describes them as evidence-informed recommendations intended to protect public health worldwide.

This corpus treats the WHO publication as health-based reference context only. Its guideline levels are not Israeli legal thresholds, US NAAQS, US AQI categories or automatic EcoGuard response triggers. This document authorizes no response action and contains no numeric guideline values; comparisons require the full source, its averaging periods and a separately reviewed rule.
