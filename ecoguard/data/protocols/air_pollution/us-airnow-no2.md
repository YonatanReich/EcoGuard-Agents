# US AirNow: ambient nitrogen-dioxide public-health guidance

## NO2: US AQI Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

US EPA AirNow's nitrogen-dioxide guide is explicitly about health protection near roadways. At the US AQI category "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" (101-150), it advises people with lung disease such as asthma, children, and older adults to limit prolonged outdoor exertion.

The reviewed international-reference recommendation is:

If an authorized source explicitly reports ambient NO2 using the US EPA AQI category Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, communicate the matching US AirNow advice that people with lung disease, children and older adults limit prolonged outdoor exertion, especially near busy roads.

This is US public-health guidance. Nearby roads are context, not proof of exposure or source attribution. Do not derive a US AQI category from Israeli AQI, EcoGuard anomaly severity, raw concentration or another reference system. The source does not support evacuation, road closure, traffic control, industrial action or dispatch.
