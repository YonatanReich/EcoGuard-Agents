# US AirNow: ground-level ozone public-health guidance

## O3: US AQI Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

US EPA AirNow identifies people with lung disease including asthma, older adults, children and teenagers, and people active outdoors as ozone-sensitive groups. At the US AQI category "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" (101-150), it advises those groups to shorten and reduce the intensity of outdoor activities, take more breaks, watch for respiratory symptoms, and plan activity when ozone is lower. It advises people with asthma to follow their asthma action plan and keep quick-relief medicine available; others should consider shorter, less intense outdoor activity.

The reviewed international-reference recommendation is:

If an authorized source explicitly reports ambient O3 using the US EPA AQI category Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, communicate the matching US AirNow advice to shorten and reduce outdoor activity intensity for ozone-sensitive groups, take breaks and follow existing asthma action plans.

This is US public-health guidance. Do not derive a US AQI category from an Israeli AQI label, EcoGuard anomaly severity, raw concentration or WHO/Israeli threshold. The source does not establish current Israeli conditions, exposure, authority acceptance, a response deadline, evacuation, closure or dispatch.
