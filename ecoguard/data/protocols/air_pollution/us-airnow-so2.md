# US EPA AirNow: ambient sulfur-dioxide public-health guidance

## SO2: US AQI Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

The US EPA AQI technical assistance document gives pollutant-specific cautionary statements. At the US AQI category "Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups" (101-150), its SO2 statement advises people with asthma to consider limiting outdoor exertion.

The reviewed international-reference recommendation is:

If an authorized source explicitly reports ambient SO2 using the US EPA AQI category Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups, communicate the matching US AirNow advice that people with asthma consider limiting outdoor exertion.

This is US AQI public-health guidance for ambient SO2. Do not infer the category from an Israeli label, EcoGuard severity, raw concentration, WHO guideline or legal standard. The guidance does not confirm exposure, identify a source or support evacuation, closure, shutdown or dispatch.
