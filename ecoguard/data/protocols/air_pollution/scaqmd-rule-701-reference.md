# South Coast AQMD Rule 701 international operational reference

## Jurisdiction and limits

South Coast Air Quality Management District Rule 701 defines episode criteria, public notification and precautionary/emergency contingency measures within that California district. The rule applies to persons within the South Coast AQMD jurisdiction and assigns determinations to its Executive Officer.

This is an international operational reference only. Its episode declarations, criteria, authority and required actions do not apply in Israel. It does not authorize EcoGuard to trigger evacuation, road closure, industrial curtailment or shutdown, school action, traffic restrictions, dispatch or any other operational measure. This document has no reviewed planner action.
