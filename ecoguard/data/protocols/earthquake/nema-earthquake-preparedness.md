# NEMA Inter-Ministerial Earthquake Preparedness

Source: National Emergency Management Authority, *Inter-Ministerial Steering
Committee for Earthquake Preparedness*.

This is a concise English rendering of the official Hebrew page. It does not
add operational dispatch quantities.

## National preparedness and rapid assessment

The committee is a professional advisory body for preparedness to respond to
earthquakes and tsunamis in Israel. It operates an expert center intended to
create a rapid situation picture and assessment immediately after an
earthquake.

The source distinguishes preparedness and scenario planning from facts about a
particular event. It states that the timing and strength of a future strong
earthquake cannot be known in advance.

## Support to local authorities and the public

The committee advances national capability to support local authorities,
including population-assistance-site kits. It also develops public guidance and
preparedness activity with implementing bodies, schools, public and private
organizations, and security coordinators.

For EcoGuard planning output, this supports retaining local-authority population
assistance as the `municipal_emergency_team` capability. It does not establish
that assistance is required at every detected event, and it supplies no number
of teams or stations.
