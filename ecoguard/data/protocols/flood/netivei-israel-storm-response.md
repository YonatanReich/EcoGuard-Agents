# Netivei Israel Stormy Weather and Flood Response Procedure

Source: Netivei Israel - National Transport Infrastructure Company[cite: 15]
Status: Official operational procedure[cite: 15]
Note: Translated and adapted from the original Hebrew document[cite: 15].

## Activation Criteria and Incident Command

* A "Stormy Weather Event" is declared by the Head of the Maintenance Division based on forecasted rain intensity, flood potential, strong winds, and snow[cite: 15].
* Upon declaration, the National Traffic Management Center (TMC) updates Variable Message Signs (VMS) on the roads to warn drivers of the impending severe weather[cite: 15].
* Regional situation reports must be transmitted every 4 hours throughout the duration of the declared event[cite: 15].

## Hazard Identification and Road Closure

* Field teams must continuously scan for and report specific hazards: road flooding, flash floods crossing traffic lanes, mud/debris accumulation, flooded water crossings/culverts, ice, fallen trees/poles, and structural road damage (e.g., sinkholes or collapsed shoulders)[cite: 15].
* **Road Closure Protocol**: Upon identifying a road-blocking hazard (such as a flood or heavy debris), the TMC shift supervisor must immediately update the Israel Police and, if necessary, request police assistance to block the route[cite: 15].
* Any physical roadblock deployed by the project manager must include predefined signage, warning measures, and safety equipment, while maintaining continuous communication with the TMC[cite: 15].

## Operational Field Response

* Heavy engineering machinery (e.g., tractors, trucks) and contractors must physically deploy to pre-designated staging areas exactly as specified in the event declaration[cite: 15].
* The field project manager must verify that all required equipment has arrived at the staging points before initiating operations[cite: 15].
* **Snow/Ice Restriction**: The scattering of salt on frozen or snowy roads must only be executed after receiving explicit approval from the Regional Manager or their deputy[cite: 15].

## Rescue and Maintenance Team Safety

* **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**: All field personnel must be equipped with a storm suit, thermal coveralls, gloves, a warm hat, a high-visibility vest, safety boots, a flashlight, and functional two-way radios[cite: 15].
* **Heavy Machinery Safety**: All heavy engineering vehicles operating in the field must be fitted with a functioning flashing beacon and a reverse warning alarm[cite: 15].
