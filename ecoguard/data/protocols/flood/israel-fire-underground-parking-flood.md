# Fire and Rescue Israel - Underground Parking Flood Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Source: Fire and Rescue Israel, Operations Division & Lehava Unit
Status: Official operational procedure
Note: Translated and adapted from the original Hebrew document.

## Initial Response and Situation Assessment

* **Initial Information Gathering**: Upon receiving an incident report, establish the structure type, its purpose, and the height/level of the flood.
* **Preliminary Actions on Scene**:
  * Link up with security guards, building managers, or residents.
  * Conduct a 360-degree survey of the building.
  * Ensure the immediate shut-off of all energy sources (electricity and gas).
  * Interview neighbors and witnesses to gather indications regarding trapped individuals in the parking lot or elevators.
  * Dispatch teams directly to elevator shafts and parking levels to search for trapped civilians.

## Force Buildup and Task Distribution

* **Resource Activation**:
  * Dispatch district YSW (Special Rescue Units - יל"מ).
  * Request local authority assistance for pumping water from the parking lot and elevator shafts.
  * Coordinate with the Israel Police for population intelligence (e.g., resident questionnaires, WhatsApp groups, security camera footage).
  * Dispatch the Lehava National Special Rescue Unit for complex operations requiring heavy urban rescue, water rescue, or specialized diving.
* **Operational Alternatives (DPAs)**:
  * *Option A*: Conduct thorough building searches for potential trapped individuals (storage rooms, apartments, elevators) and perform surface water searches using boats deployed by YSW units.
  * *Option B*: Request specialized urban flood extraction teams from the Lehava Unit.

## Safety Rules and Operational Guidelines

* **Energy Cut-Off**: Always verify that electrical and power sources to the flooded structure are fully disconnected.
* **Perimeter Control**: Block all access routes leading down to the flooded parking lot. In residential buildings, physically seal off stairwells leading to the garage to prevent children or adults from falling into flooded stairwells.
* **Elevator Protocol**:
  * If the elevator car is above the water level, cut the main power supply to the elevators immediately.
  * If the elevator is submerged in water (but not completely flooded), attempt manual lifting or open the doors using a dedicated elevator key (require an elevator technician on scene). If the doors cannot be opened and there is communication with a trapped person inside, utilize ventilation openings to supply air (via a diving cylinder and demand valve) until the elevator can be raised or the person extracted.
  * If the elevator is completely below the water line, mark it with a red warning light.
* **Sprinkler System Hazard**: Floating vehicles inside the garage can strike and break overhead sprinkler pipes, causing continuous water inflow and worsening the flood; always verify whether sprinkler systems have ruptured.
* **Intelligence Gathering for Lehava**: Prior to deploying specialized forces into flooded parking structures, gather structural drawings, confirm the number of flooded levels, and clearly mark all entry points with glow sticks (Sticklites).

## Strict Limitations on Underwater Searches in Flooded Basements

* **High Risk Warning**: Diving in flooded underground parking lots and basements is an extremely hazardous and complex operation carrying a severe risk to divers; it must be considered with extreme caution.
* **Mandatory Prerequisites for Diving**:
  1. Solid, verified intelligence indicating live trapped individuals in areas inaccessible to firefighters.
  2. Strict adherence to survival timeframes for humans submerged in cold water.
  3. **Explicit Approvals**: Emergency diving in turbid water requires the formal, personal approval of the Fire Commissioner (נציב כבאות והצלה) alongside an approved combat/entry plan authorized by the Lehava Unit Commander.
