# Ministry of Transport Flood Preparedness and Response Plan

Source: Ministry of Transport (Israel)[cite: 14]
Status: Official preparedness and adaptation plan[cite: 14]
Note: Translated and adapted from the original Hebrew document[cite: 14].

## Road Infrastructure Operations

* Deploy designated operational response teams for stormy weather at identified vulnerable hotspots to maintain traffic continuity[cite: 14].
* Monitor real-time flood alert sensors deployed at identified high-risk hotspots, such as wadi crossings and historical flood locations[cite: 14].
* Perform real-time maintenance and clearing of drainage channels and water exit zones in areas with high-frequency flooding[cite: 14].

## Railway Infrastructure Operations

* Execute predefined emergency action plans prioritizing critical railway routes and stream crossings during flood events[cite: 14].
* Protect operational depots, underground stations, and electrification and control systems (such as signaling rooms and track sensors) from inundation[cite: 14].

## Airport Operations (Ben Gurion Airport)

* Deploy on-call heavy engineering machinery and contractors capable of clearing debris, heavy mud, and obstacles, as well as pumping water[cite: 14].
* Perform real-time diversion of water from runways and operational areas[cite: 14].
* Conduct damage assessments and distribute GRF (Global Reporting Format) reports regarding precipitation conditions on runways[cite: 14].
* Execute real-time maintenance of terminal gutters and underground baggage handling systems (HBS) to prevent operational shutdowns[cite: 14].
